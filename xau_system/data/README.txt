Example CSV files for the XAUUSD local paper-mode runner

Files:
 - xau_m15.csv  (15-minute candles)
 - xau_h1.csv   (1-hour candles)
 - xau_d1.csv   (1-day candles)

Format:
timestamp,open,high,low,close,volume
2026-04-01T00:00:00Z,3025.40,3027.12,3024.83,3026.55,185

Usage:
Place these in your project's ./data folder and point your .env/config to them.
